Beverage Drink Sales Dashboard

A sales analysis dashboard for monitoring beverage sales performance and trends.

Project overview

The dashboard presents revenue, units sold, average price per unit, and profit margin, with views by beverage brand, date, region, retailer, state, and month. It also includes date and brand filters.
